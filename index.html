How we work

You own what we look at

Every review starts with a signed engagement naming exactly what may be examined and how. We only ever analyze material a client submits and is authorized to submit — never a third party's live system without their own documented, verified permission.

Source-level, not exploitation

Static analysis, secret scanning, dependency checks, and manual review of the code you give us. Nothing we do runs your application or touches a live, running system.

A human reviews every report

Findings are drafted, then reviewed, before anything is delivered. Nothing is disclosed to anyone else.

Why this matters

Most of the damage in real software incidents doesn't come from exotic zero-days. It comes from the ordinary stuff: a permission check that's missing on one code path, a secret left in a config file, a validation rule copy-pasted wrong, an assumption that held in testing and broke in production. These bugs are boring to look for and expensive to miss.

We built Mathcore around that gap — source-level review that's careful about scope and authorization, aimed at the kind of mistake that's easy to make and easy to overlook, not at chasing headline vulnerabilities in systems we don't have permission to touch.

Who this is for

Small teams and solo developers who want a second set of eyes on code before it ships, or on an existing codebase that's never had a real review — without the overhead of a large audit firm or a multi-week engagement.
