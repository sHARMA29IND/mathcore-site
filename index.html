How we work

You own what we look at

Every review starts with a signed engagement naming exactly what may be examined and how. We only ever analyze material a client submits and is authorized to submit — never a third party's live system without their own documented, verified permission.

Source-level, not exploitation

Static analysis, secret scanning, dependency checks, and manual review of the code you give us. Nothing we do runs your application or touches a live, running system.

A human reviews every report

Findings are drafted, then reviewed, before anything is delivered. Nothing is disclosed to anyone else.
