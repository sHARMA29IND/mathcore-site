Pricing

We quote every engagement individually, because the right price depends on the size and shape of what you're submitting. This page explains how we get to a number, not a fixed price list.

Bug Clinic — how it's priced

After intake, we look at the scope you've described (approximate size of the codebase, which methods you want run, and any time constraints) and send back a quote as part of the engagement agreement — before you submit any code and before anything is charged. You're never charged for a quote, and nothing is analyzed until you've accepted the price in writing.

FactorHow it affects price
Codebase sizeLarger submissions take longer to review properly
Methods requestedManual review costs more than automated scanning alone
TurnaroundRush requests, if we can accommodate them, cost more

Small software engagements — how it's priced

Fixed-price, agreed before work starts. You describe the task, we scope it precisely enough to quote a number, and that number doesn't change unless the scope does — any change in scope is discussed and re-quoted before we continue.
